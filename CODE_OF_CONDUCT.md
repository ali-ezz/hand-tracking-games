# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Use welcoming language and assume good intent.
- Encourage collaboration and constructive feedback.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or abusive language.
- Disrespect toward project maintainers or contributors.
- Disruption of community discussions or review processes.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening a private security advisory or contacting the maintainers through GitHub.
